# Tomplay Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/tomplay-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Tomplay users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Tomplay's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask their instrument and level, then say it back | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Make the 14 free days the headline | +10–15% CR to trial |
| 03 | Let them play one piece before the paywall | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Offer the month to people who close it | +5–10% ARPU |
| 05 | Lead with the proof you already have | +5–10% CR to purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Tomplay's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Tomplay. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
